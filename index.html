Help & Guidance

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything international travelers need to know when planning an unforgettable visit to Sri Lanka.

Do I need a visa to enter Sri Lanka?
Yes, most foreign nationals require an Electronic Travel Authorization (ETA/eVisa) prior to arrival. It can be conveniently applied for online through the official Sri Lankan immigration portal. We are glad to guide you through this quick process.
When is the best time of year to visit Sri Lanka?
Sri Lanka is a genuine year-round destination! Because it has two distinct monsoon patterns, when the South and West coasts experience rain (May to September), the East coast (Arugam Bay, Trincomalee) enjoys glorious sunshine. From December to April, the South and Hill Country have ideal sunny weather.
Can we customize any of the itineraries?
Absolutely! 100% of our tours are fully customizable. You can adjust the number of days, swap hotels, add extra rest days on the beach, include scenic train rides, or tailor activities according to your family's preferences.
Are your drivers licensed and English-speaking?
Yes. All our private chauffeur guides are licensed by the Sri Lanka Tourism Development Authority (SLTDA), background-checked, courteous, and speak fluent English. We can also provide German, French, or Russian speaking guides upon advance request.
How does booking and payment work?
Submitting an inquiry through our website is completely free and obligation-free. Our team will prepare a detailed day-by-day proposal with clear transparent pricing. Once you approve the itinerary, we secure bookings with a modest deposit, with the balance payable upon arrival.
